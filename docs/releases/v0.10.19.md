## Doctor repair-route fix

`aurascan doctor --fix-hung-apps` no longer raises a `NameError` after printing
the checks. The doctor entry point now accepts and initializes an explicit
`stderr` stream, and both repair routes use it. A regression test invokes the
real doctor dispatcher with `--fix-hung-apps` and verifies the stream is passed
to the repair handler.

No diagnostic evidence or repair authority changed. The wait-then-read
deadlock detector remains read-only, and a cached downgrade still requires
confirmation and the trusted sudo/pacman handoff.
