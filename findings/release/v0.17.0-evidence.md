## Retention and limits

Private signed pass receipts and the preparation receipt are retained outside
the repository. The public record carries only the hashes and the gate
outcomes.

The recovery markers prove the boot and service boundary, not storage
recovery. Encrypted Btrfs, ext4/LVM, network/offline, interrupted-package,
snapshot-confirmation and unknown-bootloader scenarios were not represented as
passing by this build. The release does not claim that a passing test proves
the whole system secure.
